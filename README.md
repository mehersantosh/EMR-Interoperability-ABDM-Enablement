# EMR Interoperability & ABDM Enablement Playbook

This repository captures practical frameworks and guidance
for enabling EMR interoperability in hospitals, aligned with
India’s Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).

It focuses on real-world hospital environments where
multiple systems, vendors, and workflows must coexist.

## Focus Areas
- EMR interoperability principles
- ABDM alignment for hospitals
- Data sharing & consent management
- Vendor-neutral architectures
- Governance and adoption challenges

## Who this is for
- Hospital CIOs / IT Heads
- Digital Health Leaders
- EMR implementation teams
- Public health & national digital programs
